A little Miami on Herron Avenue.

Miami Market is the neighborhood convenience store at 646 Herron Ave, stocked for quick stops at any hour of the day.

Come in for a late-night snack, a cold drink, a few groceries or a stack of ramen. Regulars keep coming back for the variety, the prices and the people behind the counter.

On the shelves

A tight little store with a lot packed in. Here's what you'll find when you walk through the door.

  • Ramen & noodles

    A wide ramen selection, packs and cups both.

  • Snacks & candy

    Chips, sweets and something for the walk home.

  • Cold drinks

    Grab-and-go drinks for any time of day.

  • Everyday essentials

    The basics you need, close to home.
