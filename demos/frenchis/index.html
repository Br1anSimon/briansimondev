Your corner on Atwood

Frenchi's is a deli and corner market in South Oakland, right on campus. Come in for a wrap or sub built from our deli meats, a fresh-cut gyro or a salad, and pick up snacks, drinks and the everyday stuff on your way out.

The counter stays open late, so it's here after class, after a shift, and after the party. Weekly specials go up on social at @frenchisdeli.
