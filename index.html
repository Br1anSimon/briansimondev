Process

From first conversation to launch.

  1. Talk

    We sit down and go over what your customers ask about most: hours, the menu, how to order, where to park.

  2. Design

    You see a real design of your homepage before any code gets written, and we adjust it together.

  3. Build

    I build the site and send you a preview link. Check it on your own phone and tell me what bugs you.

  4. Launch & care

    I handle the domain, hosting, and launch, then stay on for updates as your business changes.

About

It started at a restaurant where I worked the floor and ended up running the website too.

That was PowerHouse Eatery in 2020. Serving tables taught me what owners actually need from a website: the right hours, a menu that's current, and a phone number that's easy to tap. Everything else is decoration.

Today I'm studying computer science at the University of Pittsburgh and taking on web projects for local businesses. The engineering coursework keeps the code solid. The clients keep it practical.
